AI is an important part of both the development and testing of Scaffold. We explicitly encourage AI use, but it must be managed at every step. Vibe coding is not allowed.

## AI usage policy

- A human is ALWAYS responsible for their communication and code. AI is great at producing 90%-right code, but you MUST catch the remaining 10%. The only way to do this effectively is to be intimately involved at every step of the process - prompting, planning, implementation, review, and testing. You must thoroughly understand and own the end result, to a similar extent as if you were writing it yourself.
- Make an attempt to rewrite AI english output less LLM-like. AI adds lots of details - you need to trim it down to what's relevant. Make an effort for your communication to sound more like a human (yourself) than an AI.
- It's not necessary to disclose AI usage, although in some cases it may be helpful for humans reading/reviewing.
- Repeated low-effort contributors (whether using AI or not) will be blocked.
