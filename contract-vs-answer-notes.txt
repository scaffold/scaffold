/epoch/[x]
  Request /time/[x], which will block until that instant.
  Contracts with a /time/[x] input will double-check that the answer's timestamp is greater than or equal to the maximum X.
  Actually, more generally, it'll check all inputs.
  Timestamp validity check: Math.max(...contract.time_inputs) <= answer.timestamp <= Date.now()
  Future timestamps will be rejected.
  Past timestamps will fail the contract, since it requests the correct time input.
